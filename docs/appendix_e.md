## 1. Manifold Perturbation and Boundary Trapping

$$(\square_g + \mathcal{M}^2) h_{\mu\nu} = S_{\mu\nu}$$

where $\square_g$ is the d'Alembertian operator in 3+1 spacetime and $\mathcal{M}$ represents the mass-energy effective coupling constant of the interface. In the limit of a compactified hyperdimensional cavity of length $L$, the resonance modes $\omega_n$ are quantized according to the boundary topology:

$$\omega_n = \frac{n \pi c}{L} \sqrt{1 - \beta^2}$$

$$\delta f = \frac{1}{2\pi} \oint_{\Gamma} \nabla_\mu (D_{ij} h^{ij}) dt$$
