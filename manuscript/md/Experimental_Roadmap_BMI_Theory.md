# Experimental Roadmap: Validating the Bimodal Manifold Interaction (BMI) Theory

* **Target:** Torsion-balance experiments (e.g., Eöt-Wash group).
* **Goal:** Detect force-gradient spikes as separation approaches $\delta$.

* **Target:** High-resolution CMB datasets (Simons Observatory / CMB-S4).
* **Goal:** Confirm B-mode polarization patterns exhibit statistical alignment with the preferred vector $n_i$ derived from the hyperspatial shear.

* **Target:** Next-generation Event Horizon Telescope (EHT) imaging.
* **Goal:** Identify non-circularity or asymmetric intensity profiles in the photon ring of supermassive black holes (Sgr A*) inconsistent with the vacuum Kerr metric.

## 4. Bimodal Decay-Width Anomalies (High-Precision Spectroscopy)
